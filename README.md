# Medieval Arms and Armor Research

A research reference project compiling detailed metadata for selected pieces from
**The Metropolitan Museum of Art's Arms and Armor collection** (Department ID: 4),
sourced from the [Met Museum Collection API](https://metmuseum.github.io/).

## Overview

A keyword search for "medieval armor sword" within the Arms and Armor department,
filtered to objects with images, returned **386 object IDs**. This project documents
a representative sample of **10 pieces** — helmets, full armors, horse armor, shields,
gauntlets, and arm defenses — spanning European and Japanese arms-making traditions,
with metadata, dimensions, image links, and research notes.

## Contents

- [`met-arms-armor-reference.md`](./met-arms-armor-reference.md) — the main research
  reference document: catalogue of selected pieces, a quick-reference summary table,
  research notes (methodology, materials, sub-collections, geographic/chronological
  spread), and next steps for research.

## Data Source

All metadata reflects records returned by the
[Met Museum Collection API](https://metmuseum.github.io/) at the time of compilation.
The Arms and Armor department corresponds to Department ID `4`.
